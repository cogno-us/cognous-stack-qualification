## Findings
### F-A1: operation substitution bypasses the F-01 policy-containment check: candidate HIGH (reproduced by the lead; Architect to adjudicate)
- **Owner:** `cogno-us/cognous-execution-runtime` (`SelectionAuthorityStore.check_effect`) and the r08-v2 contract (Amendment 3 does not list it as a residual limit; manifest AU-7 expected a refusal).
- **Setup:** one store with two validly activated operations: `op-transfer` (narrow record) and `op-other` (wide record), same tenant, institution, authority domain and consumer. Claim derived from a real Control Plane workflow-issued claim; the executor policy is the WIDE sets.
- **Control:** wide policy with a binding naming `op-transfer` (commitment recomputed): denied, `policy_identity_mismatch`, cause `policy_not_subset:allowed_actions`, 0 effects.
- **Probe:** the same wide policy with the binding rewritten to `op-other` (commitment recomputed): **executed**, 1 effect, 1 attempt, 1 receipt.
- **Cause:** the guard builds the governed scope from the binding alone, and the envelope carries no Registry operation label to compare with it. The Registry "operation" label exists only in the claim-supplier-controlled binding, so a supplier can choose which activated record bounds the policy.
- **Preconditions and limits:** a claim-supplier or mis-composed-issuance capability (the same threat class as F-01); an ordinary external caller cannot do this; compromise of normal Control Plane issuance was NOT demonstrated (the Control Plane refuses a binding its approval did not commit to: AU-1 companion HELD). The committed effect is the operation the workflow actually authorized.
- **Why candidate HIGH:** the Architect classified F-01 HIGH because the Runtime was not an independent enforcement point for Registry narrowing independent of the claim supplier. F-A1 is the same property through the scope dimension. The lead proposes HIGH and does not self-downgrade.
- **Remediation direction (not implemented):** bind the Registry operation to a host-trusted source at the destination (for example a host-configured expected consumer and operation compared with the binding at the guard, mirroring the Control Plane's issuance-time `expected_consumer_id`/`expected_operation`), with a contract amendment. Requires a new Architect directive.
- **Provenance note:** the probe was added post hoc by Worker A after its pre-declared AU-7 operation-substitution case was recorded CHARACTERIZED; it is disclosed as a deviation and the lead reproduced it independently of the worker.
