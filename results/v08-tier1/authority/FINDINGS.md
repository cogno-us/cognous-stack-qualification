## F-A1 (BREACH, post-hoc oracle): operation substitution bypasses the F-01 policy-containment check

Owner: Runtime `engine/registry_selection_authority.py` `check_effect` (the governed scope, hence the activation row and the record used for containment, is built from the claim binding alone, including `binding.operation`; the execution envelope carries no operation member) together with the r08-v2 contract / Amendment 3 (which does not state this as a residual limit; Amendment 3 section 3 lists three limits and this is not one of them).
Reproduction (test `test_au7_operation_substitution_bypasses_policy_containment_posthoc`, evidence in `runs/run-002.json`): one store with two validly activated operations of the same tenant, institution, authority domain and consumer: `op-transfer` (record = exactly the sets the proposal needs) and `op-other` (record = those sets plus one extra member in each of the five). The executor policy is the wide sets of op-other's record. Control: a claim derived from the workflow-issued claim whose binding names `op-transfer`, with the wide `effective_policy_digest` and a recomputed commitment, is denied `policy_identity_mismatch` / REFUSE cause `policy_not_subset:allowed_actions`, 0 effects. Probe: the same claim with binding.operation, selection_id, selection_epoch, selection_digest, record triple and activation_ref rewritten to op-other's activation (commitment recomputed) was provisioned and EXECUTED: 1 effect, 1 attempt, 1 receipt, guard `binding_verified` against `sel-test-op2`.
Impact and honest limits: the committed effect is the operation the Control Plane workflow actually authorized, and the evaluated policy still admits it; what is bypassed is the claim that the executor policy is contained in the record activated for THAT operation (a colluding claim supplier chooses which activated record bounds the policy). It requires a supplier able to forge a claim (the F-01 threat model) and a second activated operation in the same tenant/institution/domain/consumer. The oracle was declared AFTER seeing the CHARACTERIZED result of the earlier operation-substitution test (that one was pre-declared CHARACTERIZED in the inventory); manifest AU-7 states "operation substitution refuses", which this does not meet at the effect boundary. Classification and severity are for the lead and the Architect to adjudicate. Per the stop condition I did not expand AU-7 further and did not repair anything.

## Characterized / observations
* `AtomicLocalControlPlaneExecutor.provision_claim` does not forward `policy_binding`, `expected_consumer_id` or `expected_operation`; a successor claim can only be issued through `provision_local_execution_claim` called directly with the destination callback (this is how AU-1 was run). Not a breach; a usability gap that explains why earlier work could not exercise workflow issuance through the executor helper.
* Consistent institution substitution is stopped by the untyped claim/envelope check before the selection guard is reached (no typed `selection_refusal`).
* The shared conftest budget hook counts every collected item before `-k` deselection; node ids must be passed to run a subset.
