## World mapping

Every grant of the declared Wiki world is provisioned at the destination the way the registered fixture provisions its own grant: policy "policy1", target "sink", principal "agent", the lifetime (expires_at) and the effect count (max_effects) of the world's grant record, one fence generation. A destination grant pins one target, so a call to any other target is outside every grant (rule R2). The 15 grants that are provisioned in addition to the registered one use 15 of the 128 admission attempts of the first interval of 60 clock units (interface item DEST-INGRESS); the largest executable scenario of the plan file makes 101 calls.

## Rules

- R1. A step is applied to the destination exactly when its declared interface can express it (ocs_column.classify_step). A scenario with a step that cannot be expressed is declared NOT-EXPRESSIBLE, citing the interface item that lacks the field, operation or state and the sentence of the case that the scenario was written from.
- R2. Expressible steps: execute through the eight request fields to the one target that a destination grant pins; a clock step; the zero-argument controls revoke, fence, rotate, reopen and break_audit; revoke_grant:<grant> for a grant of the declared world. Every other control, and every call to another target, has no counterpart.
- R3. A scenario whose steps are all expressible but whose question is carried in issuer_claims or in payload content (which the destination records and never reads) is declared NOT-CLAIMED only when a sentence published before the plan states what OCS does not claim for the case (a not-claimed sentence of the applicability file at its pinned revision, or amendment 39); it is still run and its observation is retained apart from the scored results (amendment 74). Without such a sentence the declaration would be void and the scenario is ASKED.
- R4. Any other scenario is ASKED and scored by the same oracle as the comparator applications.
- R5. A declaration does not depend on the expected statuses of the scenario and is made before any result exists.
