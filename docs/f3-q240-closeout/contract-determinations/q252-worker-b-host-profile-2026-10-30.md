## 2. Why the host digest is isolable ONLY by masking (INFERENCE, derivation from F3-F8, F17)
Effective profile = f(host, Registry). A fixture isolates `host_profile_digest` iff h1 != h2 and f(h1,R) == f(h2,R), i.e. f is non-injective on the edited field. `compose_profile` and `compose_unreadable` pass every non-governed host field through unchanged (`doc = host.model_dump(...)`), so any host edit outside a Registry-overwritten field also moves the effective profile. Therefore every isolating host fixture is a masked-host fixture by construction, and an "unmasked-host control" cannot be an isolating fixture; at most it is a specificity/contrast control. That is why record 12 asks to "state the masked-host limit alongside": the masking is the isolation device, not an incidental caveat.
